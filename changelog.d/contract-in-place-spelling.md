Draft change. `spec/contract.md` KISS-CONTRACT-6.5-0009 pinned no spelling for the Interface `in_place` field: its prose said `none` while the golden document said `false`, and nothing spelled an aliased input. It is now exactly `none` or `input:<k>`, `<k>` the 0-based index of the aliased operand among the input operands in the canonical operand order (KISS-CLASSIFY-6.6-0014), decimal without leading zeros; any other spelling is a typed decline. The golden document (`conformance/corpus/contract_vectors.json`, the Appendix C Interface block, `conformance/src/contract.rs`) says `in_place = none`, so its body length and crc32 move. New reader `parse_in_place` and `SectionDecline::BadFieldValue` in `conformance/src/contract.rs`, and the test `test_contract_in_place_declared` in `conformance/tests/contract_section_schemas.rs`, which backs the clause for the first time (the unbacked ledger and the coverage floor move: harness 538 to 539, untested 391 to 390).
