**Breaking** Draft change to KISS-Ops, one re-emit for consumers (CireSnave boards 426 6 "A", 427 "2) d)" and "1: A", 2026-11-12). (1) `matmul` becomes a carrier op: its OpAttrs blob, empty until now, is three bytes `accumulator`, `math_fidelity`, `precision_tag` (`spec/ops.md` KISS-OPS-6.19-0049, the §6.19.3 row, the carrier lists); a `matmul` over an integer compute dtype may take a signed 32- or 64-bit integer accumulator, summed exactly and exact-byte (KISS-OPS-6.17-0010, relaxing the float-only wording of 6.17-0008 for `matmul`); `reduce` is unchanged. (2) A `precision_tag` sub-vocabulary `{1=none, 2=3xtf32, 3=fp8-fast-accumulate}` in the maintainer-owned vocabulary, with the duty to declare a bound and no recipe pinned (KISS-OPS-6.17-0011). (3) The `reduce` `monoid` enum gains `5=welford`, `6=online-softmax`, `7=argmax` (KISS-OPS-6.19-0014), each returning one packed tensor with a trailing state axis, with state, identity, merge, finalise, layout and vectors (KISS-OPS-6.11-0026 to 6.11-0029; a rank-`MAX_RANK` operand with keepdim declines). (4) KISS-CLASSIFY-6.6-0025: a tuple-state reduction is presented without its state axis, so no `structure_key` byte moves; a state axis left in declines with `StateAxisPresented`. New `conformance/src/packed_reduce.rs`, `conformance/corpus/packed_reduction_vectors.json`, `conformance/tests/packed_reduce.rs`, `conformance/tests/matmul_attrs.rs`, `conformance/tests/classify_packed_presenter.rs`; the OpAttrs vectors `conformance/corpus/opattrs_vectors.json` and `conformance/corpus/opattrs_value_vectors.json` regenerated (population 295 to 308).
