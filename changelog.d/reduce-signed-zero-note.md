Two PM-ruled semantics edits to `spec/ops.md`, no stored byte moves. KISS-OPS-6.0-0002 gains an exception: a float `max`/`min` reduce or scan returning a zero from folded +0.0 and -0.0 yields a zero, value-equal (byte-equality not promised), matching `canon_signed_zero` in `conformance/src/structural.rs`. KISS-OPS-6.11-0004 pins the outcome of a read decline (delivered before the result can be consumed; flag, pre-validation or op-not-offered all conformant; the declined lane's value unspecified, never a fault) and 6.11-0005 states that every `scatter`-decomposed write op (`scatter_add`, a later `index_add`) skips an out-of-range write. Decision notes: `docs/options-reduce-max-min-signed-zero.md`, `docs/options-oob-index-gaps.md`. No new clause id, codec field, token or corpus vector.
