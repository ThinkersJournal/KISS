kind: **BREAKING**: vulkan vocabulary 8 names the four OCP Microscaling component types
---
Vocabulary version 7 to 8 in `spec/namespaces/vulkan.md` (V-17): `f8e8m0`, `f6e2m3`, `f6e3m2` and `f4e2m1` now spell the `VK_EXT_shader_ocp_microscaling_types` component types (registry extension 673, `VK_HEADER_VERSION` 368) in a `cm-` tuple. A device that reports a tuple using one of them gains it in its `vulkan:` token (version 7 omitted it whole under V-7), so cached version 7 tokens for such devices stop matching; the asserted version (KISS-CLASSIFY-6.8-0009) attributes it. `VK_COMPONENT_TYPE_MXINT8_EXT` stays unnamed (no KISS dtype; a GAP row in `docs/dtype-completeness-audit.md`). `conformance/corpus/structure_key_vectors.json`, `conformance/src/reference_vectors.rs` and `conformance/tests/classify_unnamed_types.rs` move to 8. Scheduled on the pm-kernels ruling of 2026-11-19 (vulkane-kernel-emit-8b-2); the `<arith>` field is unchanged. Also corrects stale schema-version text: `spec/classify.md` KISS-CLASSIFY-6.6-0004, its 6.6 field table and the section 8 introduction said 5 (and a test comment said 4) while KISS-CLASSIFY-6.4-0003 and the `test_classify_structure_key_field_layout` assertions say 8 (sk8); no token byte moves. Adds `docs/options-r26-linalg-factorization.md` (ROADMAP R2.6 options note, informative: no clause, nothing built; Fuel confirmed no consumer).
