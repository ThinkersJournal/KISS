---
CireSnave's 2026-11-21 rulings on boards 475 and 476 ("476: Go with your recommendations for the rest."; "Rather than making the two internal fields optional, remove them. KISS should provide no way to enforce anything on the internals of a kernel."). **KISS-Kernel is deleted**: `spec/kernel.md`, its sidecar, `tools/kiss_kernel_refs.py` with its test, and `conformance/tests/kernel_refs.rs`; KISS-KERNEL-6.1-0001 and KISS-KERNEL-8-0001 are retired and their IDs are never reused. The "Kernel-governed" tags and the 6.22 table leave KISS-Ops (the scatter atomic order and reach are no longer stated anywhere in KISS; Ops keeps only the pinned results), the umbrella and README lose the KISS-Kernel row, and the three board 450/459 working documents are marked superseded. `conformance/corpus/op_facts.json` is now schema `kiss-op-facts-v2` with the `kernel_rules` field removed (a reader that hard-rejects `kiss-op-facts-v1` must accept v2). **Contract, `contract_version` 4 to 5** (KISS-CONTRACT-6.1-0008, 8-0002): the Dispatch section declares exactly `{invocation_domain, workgroup_sizing, count_to_grid}` or the geometry-agnostic sentinel; `thread_mapping`, `addressing_rule` and the grid-stride-only rule are removed, so KISS-CONTRACT-6.6-0004 and 6.6-0008 are retired (IDs burned, tests `test_contract_thread_and_addressing` and `test_contract_reserved_tile_mapping` removed). Added: optional Capabilities field `requires_co_resident_launch` (6.7-0007, pinned after `cost`, `true` or `false`, absent is `false`), optional Guarantees field `entry_exit_visibility` (6.8-0014, pinned after `cost_provenance`, `caller-ordered` or `visible-at-exit`, absent is `caller-ordered`, token spelling is the spec author's, CireSnave has not seen it), and a short visibility table with one clause (6.5-0020). No read/write footprint field was added (CireSnave: no). **Announce** (KISS-ANNOUNCE-6.6-0012): every device record gains a trailing u8 `cooperative_launch` (1 offered, 0 not), so every `DVRS` body changes. The kiss_trace ratchet gains `base_proven_map_from_ref` so retiring a proven clause is a recorded move, not a regression. Corpora `contract_vectors.json` and `announce_vectors.json` are regenerated. The two scope and placement notes (boards 474 and 475) are folded in. Caveat: the control rules (barrier, total integer division, atomic old value) exist in no audited spec, so the audit of other specs had no in-spec positive control.
