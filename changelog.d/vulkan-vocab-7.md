kind: **BREAKING** (namespace `vulkan:` vocabulary 6 -> 7): `<arith>` names `a64`, `af32`, `af64`
---
`spec/namespaces/vulkan.md` (maintainer-owned annex, KISS-CLASSIFY-6.8-0004) moves to vocabulary version 7 as the maintainer (vulkane) proposed it: three `<arith>` atomics names (`a64` = 64-bit integer atomics on buffer AND shared memory, `af32`/`af64` = buffer atomic float add), new V-16 forbidding inference between them and `i64`/`f64`, a section-4 baseline row and version-7 paragraph. All three feature bits predate the baseline (`VK_HEADER_VERSION` 348), so the section-4 rule bumps the version as written. A device that has 64-bit atomics changes token (`arith-dot8-f16` becomes `arith-a64-dot8-f16`). `conformance/src/reference_vectors.rs` `NAMESPACE_VOCAB_VERSIONS` and the `namespace_vocabulary_versions` stamp in `conformance/corpus/structure_key_vectors.json` move 6 to 7 (one line; arity stays 5); `conformance/tests/classify_unnamed_types.rs` follows. Verified: `cargo test` in `conformance/` has no failing result line; the ratchet floor does not move (561/44/384, proven 24). Sample tokens and the `arith` alphabet of the namespace manifest are regenerated by vulkane after this lands.
