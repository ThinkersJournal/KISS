CireSnave's 2026-11-21 rulings A1-A7 and C2, applied in `spec/ops.md` and `spec/emit.md`. A1: KISS-OPS-6.5-0004 (no mask-multiply rewrite of `select`) is removed and its ID is burned, with `test_ops_select_no_mask_multiply`; the raw-bit result stays pinned by KISS-OPS-6.5-0002. A2: KISS-OPS-6.10-0006 drops the truncate-or-direct-load menu and keeps the truncated result and independence from DAG sharing. A3: KISS-OPS-6.13-0004 drops the two-pass/Welford algorithm and keeps `reduce_var >= +0` and no NaN in `reduce_std` through cancellation. A4: KISS-OPS-6.18-0006 drops the ban on routing `cdiv` through real `div` and keeps the Annex G results and the pinned infinity signs. A5: KISS-OPS-6.18-0012 drops the composition of governed `clog`/`cmul`/`cexp` and keeps the principal value, `cpow(z, 0) = 1 + 0i` and the three zero-base results. A6: KISS-OPS-6.18-0015 binds the component dtype only for exact-byte complex ops; a ULP/tolerance op is bound by its declared tolerance. A7: KISS-OPS-6.21-0002 drops the intermediate-dtype sentence and keeps single rounding of the exact source value. C2: KISS-EMIT-6.4-0001, -0002 and -0003 drop the neutral-driver-MUST-NOT halves; the emitter-must-supply placement (C1) stays. The mask-multiply test and the "mandated polynomial" assertion of `test_emit_transcendental_spelling_emitter_supplied` are removed; every outcome test is unchanged. D1 (PM approved the three rewrites as proposed): KISS-SYNTH-6.1-0004a now says a request whose declared length exceeds the remaining input draws a typed decline and no other effect; KISS-SYNTH-6.6-0001b says a provider's response depends only on the bytes the framing declares; KISS-SYNTH-6.3-0002 drops the word "build" (6.3-0003 and `test_synth_build_on_miss_is_jit` unchanged); the tests are kept. B1: KISS-CONTRACT-6.8-0012 drops the calibration-record requirement (method, corpus, margin of at least 1) and `KISS-OPS-6.11-0024`'s pointer to it; the declared ULP-of-A band, `k` declared per kernel, the absolute band and the exact-equality zero band stand, and a kernel may now declare `k` without a record. K3 is not adopted; its measurement is an addendum to `docs/options-r1-16-reassociation-band.md`. Ambiguity: the ruling said to remove the "3rd sentence" of KISS-OPS-6.18-0006; by count the means sentence (no routing through real `div`) is the 2nd, and that is the one removed, its pinned-infinity-sign outcome kept. Not touched: D2, D3, board 476.
