kind: contract vectors for the op_dag and Identity consistency (additive, draft-tracking)
---
Adds the `op_dag_vectors` key to `conformance/corpus/contract_vectors.json`: 10 whole-document accept vectors and 29 single-fault decline vectors for KISS-CONTRACT-6.11-0007, 6.4-0002, 6.4-0007 and 6.3-0006, requested by Fireline (fireline#60, before it wires its op_dag reader into `read_contract`). Declines carry a `(section, tag)` pair: `semantics` with `op-dag-malformed`, `op-dag-bad-source`, `op-dag-child-not-earlier`, `op-dag-multiple-roots` (root not last), `op-dag-unknown-op`, `op-dag-empty`, `bad-semantics-kind`; `record` with `op-identity-root-mismatch` and `cell-class-unsupported-by-op`. The accept set covers `ctr`, `ndx` and `rdc` on ops that declare the fact (a carrier, a scan through its inner carrier, `argmax`, a pool), a fusion whose only contraction is a non-root op, every `Source` tag with a `Bind` node, a shape-decided class that asks nothing of the op, and an empty `declared-op-tag` DAG. The class check reads `conformance/corpus/op_facts.json`, generated from `spec/ops.md`; there is no per-op table in the reader.

The reference reader gains the rules: `conformance/src/op_dag.rs` (parse, order, `Source` spelling, op names, identity and cell-class assertion) is called from `section_vectors::read_full_document`, and the generator states each vector's expected pair, so a vector the reader does not decline as stated fails the build. Rulings pinned in the artifact's `rulings_pinned` note, for the PM and CireSnave to confirm or reverse: (1) a `machine-checkable-IR` Semantics with an empty `op_dag` is declined (a complete IR has a root, 6.4-0007 and 6.4-0002); (2) a `declared-op-tag` Semantics may carry an empty `op_dag` and is then exempt from the 6.3-0006 assertion, and is checked like any DAG when it has nodes; (3) for a fusion the class requirement is met by any `Op` node, never by a `Source` or `Bind`; (4) the `op_identity` is compared to the root by bare op name only: identity-bearing attributes and the operand-role tuple are not checked by these vectors. Tests: `conformance/tests/contract_op_dag.rs` backs `KISS-CONTRACT-6.11-0007`, `6.4-0002`, `6.4-0007` and `6.3-0006` (the last two of the three rows leave `conformance/UNBACKED.tsv`). Additive: no existing vector or tag changes; vendors re-vendor `contract_vectors.json`.
